## Core mission

RestaurantOS AI should not become just another POS system like Petpooja. Our
goal is to become the **Restaurant Growth Operating System**.

Every feature we build must solve at least one of these real business
problems:

- Increase restaurant revenue
- Reduce operational costs
- Save staff time
- Improve customer satisfaction
- Increase repeat customers
- Reduce food waste
- Help owners make better decisions

If a feature doesn't achieve one of these goals, it should not be
prioritized.

## Restaurant owner features

### 1. AI Restaurant Manager
Instead of showing dashboards, the system proactively tells the owner what
needs attention: revenue lower than usual, kitchen delays, slow-moving
dishes, low stock alerts, staff performance, a daily business summary, sales
prediction. The owner should receive actionable insights every morning
instead of searching through reports.

### 2. AI Dynamic Pricing
Recommend menu price changes based on demand, profit margins, and customer
behavior — raise prices where demand stays strong, reduce prices on
slow-selling dishes, suggest profitable combos.

### 3. Predictive Inventory
Predict future requirements instead of only showing current stock: expected
weekend demand, recommended purchase quantities, low-stock prediction,
ingredient forecasting, seasonal demand analysis.

### 4. Smart Waitlist
Beyond a simple queue number: predict waiting time, notify customers via
WhatsApp, recommend nearby places while they wait, automatically call
customers when a table frees up.

### 5. Customer Memory
Maintain customer preferences across visits — favorite dishes, allergies,
spice preference, birthday, anniversary, VIP recognition, average spending.
Creates premium customer service.

### 6. AI Complaint Detection
Detect unhappy customers before they leave a bad review: long waiting time,
returned dishes, multiple waiter calls, delayed serving, low ratings —
immediately notify the manager.

### 7. Automated Marketing
Intelligent, not spam: identify customers who haven't visited recently,
high-value customers, birthday offers, personalized dish recommendations,
festival campaigns.

### 8. Smart Staff Scheduling
Predict staff requirements from expected customer traffic — weekend
staffing, holiday staffing, peak-hour recommendations, shift optimization.

### 9. Voice Order Entry
Staff create orders by voice — "Two Butter Naan, One Paneer Tikka, One
Coke" — automatically converted into a KOT.

### 10. Table Intelligence
Track table performance: average occupancy time, revenue per table, average
bill, table turnover, most profitable tables.

### 11. AI Upselling
Recommend additional dishes from ordering patterns — frequently bought
together, combo suggestions, dessert/beverage recommendations.

### 12. Restaurant Reputation Dashboard
Monitor reputation across platforms — Google Reviews, Zomato, Swiggy,
WhatsApp feedback, internal feedback — in one place.

### 13. Competitor Intelligence
Help owners understand nearby competition: price comparison, popular
dishes, rating comparison, trending food categories.

### 14. Staff Performance Analytics
Measure staff quality with business metrics: orders handled, customer
ratings, tips, complaint ratio, upselling performance.

### 15. Kitchen AI
Prioritize cooking based on waiting time: which order should cook first,
delay prediction, kitchen workload balancing.

### 16. Food Waste Analytics
Reduce wastage using historical data: frequently wasted ingredients, daily
waste reports, purchase optimization.

### 17. Revenue Leak Detection
Automatically detect financial problems: cash mismatch, suspicious refunds,
frequent discounts, cancelled orders, unauthorized modifications.

### 18. Multi-Branch Intelligence
For owners with multiple restaurants: best/worst performing branch, branch
comparison, manager performance, revenue comparison.

### 19. AI Business Coach
Owners ask natural-language questions — "Why are sales down?", "Which
dishes should I remove?", "Which dishes should I promote?", "Why are
customers complaining?" — and the AI answers using the restaurant's own
data.

## Customer experience features

### 1. QR-based ordering
Customers never install an app: QR scan → browser → menu → order → payment.
*(Shipped — see `12_PRODUCT_SCOPE.md` build order item 3 and
`CLAUDE.md` for how it was built.)*

### 2. Rich food information
Every dish includes real photos, a preparation video, taste profile, spice
level, ingredients, nutrition, allergens, dietary information — so
customers can confidently order unfamiliar food.

### 3. AI dish recommendations
Natural-language search — "I want spicy vegetarian food," "I need high
protein," "Suggest something for kids."

### 4. Group ordering
Multiple customers scan the same table QR; everyone adds items; the
restaurant receives one shared order.

### 5. Smart bill splitting
Friends split payment individually; the restaurant receives one completed
bill.

### 6. Live order tracking
Order accepted → preparing → ready → serving → completed, like food-delivery
tracking.

### 7. Family profiles
Remember preferences for an entire family — a child who likes fries, a
vegetarian member, a peanut allergy, favorite dishes.

### 8. AI menu translation
Translate menus into multiple languages (English, Hindi, Marathi, Arabic,
Japanese, French, …) for tourists and international customers.

### 9. Waiter assistance
Call waiter, request water/tissues/bill/assistance — no waving for
attention.

### 10. Digital feedback
Only verified customers (those who actually ordered) can rate; feedback is
requested automatically via WhatsApp right after payment.

## Future AI vision

RestaurantOS AI should become an intelligent operating system that
continuously learns from restaurant operations, able to answer questions
like:

- What should I prepare tomorrow?
- Which dishes generate the highest profit?
- Which staff members perform best?
- Why did revenue decrease?
- Which customers are likely to return?
- Which customers are about to churn?
- What inventory should I purchase today?
- Which menu items should be removed?
- What promotions should I run this weekend?

The long-term goal is to become the restaurant owner's **AI business
partner**, helping them make better decisions every day.

## Product philosophy

RestaurantOS AI is not just a POS system. It is a complete **Restaurant
Growth Operating System** focused on four outcomes:

1. Increase revenue
2. Reduce costs
3. Improve customer experience
4. Help restaurant owners make better decisions

Every new feature should directly contribute to at least one of these four
goals.
